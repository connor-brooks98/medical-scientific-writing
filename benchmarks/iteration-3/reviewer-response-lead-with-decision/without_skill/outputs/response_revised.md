# Response to Reviewers

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We have added this. The CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and Results (page 9, lines 182–184) now report loss to follow-up by group: 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 (5.0%) in the control group. The original submission gave only the overall figure.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We have kept the 90-day endpoint and added our rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). We considered both endpoints during trial design. Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within 90 days, and the intervention was designed to act during the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would also have required a larger sample size than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We agree and have revised the Discussion accordingly. Because the trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center, we now limit our conclusions to that population treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306). We have also added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
