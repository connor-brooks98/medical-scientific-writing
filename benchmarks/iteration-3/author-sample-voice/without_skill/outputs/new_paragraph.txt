Notably, our findings highlight the crucial importance of pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge, underscoring its pivotal role in reducing adverse drug events. In this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, the intervention reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). Furthermore, the effect was not only statistically significant but also clinically meaningful, representing a paradigm shift in transitions of care. Interestingly, the effect was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points), which serves as a testament to the value of targeting high-risk patients.
