Pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events. In this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, the 30-day rate fell from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). We think a reduction of this size matters clinically, not just statistically. The effect was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points) — a subgroup result, and one we would not overinterpret. Still, it suggests the intervention may do the most good in patients on complex regimens.
