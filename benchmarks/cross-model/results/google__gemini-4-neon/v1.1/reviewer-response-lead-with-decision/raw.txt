# Response to reviewers

Thank you for your comments. Below are our responses and the changes made to the manuscript.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We agree and have added the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We have kept the 90-day primary endpoint and added our rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days, and the intervention was designed to act during this transition period. A 1-year endpoint would also have required a larger sample size than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We agree. Because our trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center, we have revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to this population treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306). We also added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
