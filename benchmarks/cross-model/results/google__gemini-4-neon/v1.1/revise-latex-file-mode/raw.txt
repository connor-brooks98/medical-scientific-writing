\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

A total of 2,418 patients met the inclusion criteria (Figure~\ref{fig:flow}). After propensity-score matching \cite{austin2011introduction}, baseline characteristics were balanced between groups (Table~\ref{tab:baseline}).

The primary outcome occurred in 142 patients (11.7\%) in the intervention group and 198 patients (16.4\%) in the control group (hazard ratio [HR] 0.69; 95\% CI 0.56--0.86; $p = 0.001$). This association was consistent across all prespecified subgroups (Figure~\ref{fig:forest}).

The adjusted model was specified as
\begin{equation}
  \log h_i(t) = \log h_0(t) + \beta_1 \text{Treatment}_i + \boldsymbol{\gamma}^\top \mathbf{x}_i ,
  \label{eq:cox}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{x}_i$ denotes the vector of baseline covariates listed in Table~\ref{tab:baseline} \cite{cox1972regression, therneau2000modeling}.

Among secondary outcomes, all-cause mortality did not differ significantly between the intervention and control groups (7.9\% versus 8.6\%; HR 0.92; 95\% CI 0.70--1.21).
