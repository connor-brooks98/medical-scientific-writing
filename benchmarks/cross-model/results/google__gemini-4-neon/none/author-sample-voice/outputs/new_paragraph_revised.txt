In this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). The effect is clinically meaningful. Among patients taking 10 or more medications, the benefit was larger — a risk difference of -6.1 percentage points — which supports targeting high-risk patients during transitions of care.
