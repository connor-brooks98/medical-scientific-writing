# Response to Reviewers

We thank the reviewers for their comments.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We added the number lost to follow-up in each group to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints during trial design. Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days, and the intervention was designed for the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample size than our funding permitted. We kept the 90-day endpoint and added this rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125).

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center. We revised the Discussion to limit the conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
