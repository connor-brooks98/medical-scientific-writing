## Five highest-priority revisions

1. Remove the claim that early norepinephrine prevents death; the retrospective result is an association.
2. Support or qualify the earlier survival claim using identifiable studies and their designs.
3. Define the knowledge gap and explain how it follows from the existing evidence.
4. State the prospective study’s question and design, including the comparator and outcome.
5. Clarify the retrospective analysis, particularly its comparator, adjustment, timing definitions, and potential biases.

The supplied numerical result is an adjusted odds ratio of 0.78 (95% CI, 0.64–0.94) in 2,914 patients; its source and supporting analysis were not available for this audit.
