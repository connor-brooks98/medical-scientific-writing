# Response to Reviewers

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We have added loss to follow-up by group to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group. The original submission reported overall loss to follow-up.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We have kept the 90-day endpoint and added the rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). We considered both 90-day and 1-year endpoints during trial design. Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days, and the intervention was designed to act during the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample size than was feasible within our funding.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We have limited the Discussion's conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325). The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center.
