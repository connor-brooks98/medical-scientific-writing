Two findings deserve comment. First, in our cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). We consider this difference clinically meaningful. Second, the effect was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points).
