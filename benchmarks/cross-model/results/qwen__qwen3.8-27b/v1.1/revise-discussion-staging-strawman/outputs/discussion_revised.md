## Discussion

In this retrospective cohort of 1,146 patients with dementia, mobilization within 24 hours of hip fracture surgery was associated with lower 30-day mortality (6.2% vs 9.8%; adjusted odds ratio, 0.61; 95% CI, 0.39–0.95) and shorter length of stay (median 8 vs 11 days).

This association does not establish that early mobilization improves cognition. Delirium incidence did not differ between groups (31% vs 29%); a cohort of this size may not detect small differences in delirium, so the study cannot resolve whether mobilization affects cognition.

The design cannot determine whether the association is causal or whether it extends to patients with advanced dementia; a randomized trial of early versus delayed mobilization after hip fracture surgery in patients with dementia, with 30-day mortality as the primary outcome, would address the first question.
