Two findings deserve comment. First, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events. In this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, the intervention reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). Second, the effect was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points), a pattern consistent with targeting patients at higher risk. We did not design the trial to compare subgroups, so the latter finding should be interpreted cautiously.
