# Response to Reviewers

We thank the reviewers for their comments. Our responses follow each comment; page and line numbers refer to the revised manuscript.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We have added the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and to the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints during the design phase. Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days, and the intervention was designed to act during the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would also have required a larger sample size than our funding supported. We have added this rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125).

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We agree. The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center, and the original wording extended the findings more broadly. We have revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
