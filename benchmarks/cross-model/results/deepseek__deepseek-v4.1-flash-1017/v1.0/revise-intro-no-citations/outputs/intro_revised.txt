Introduction

Smartwatches that combine photoplethysmography (PPG) with single-lead electrocardiography (ECG) can record a rhythm trace away from the clinic, and several consumer devices now include an AF detection feature [CITATION NEEDED: consumer devices with an AF detection feature, and their regulatory status]. How well that feature performs in the oldest patients is a separate question from how well it performs in the populations in which it was validated.
