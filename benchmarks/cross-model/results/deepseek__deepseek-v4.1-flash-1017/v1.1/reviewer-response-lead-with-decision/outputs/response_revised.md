# Response to Reviewers

We thank both reviewers for their comments. Each response below states the change and its location in the revised manuscript.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We agree. The original submission reported only overall loss to follow-up; we have added the number in each group to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and to the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** The 90-day endpoint was chosen at the design stage. We have kept it and added the rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days [CITATION NEEDED: proportion of readmissions within 90 days], and the intervention was designed to act during the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would also have required a larger sample size than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We agree that the original wording extended the findings too broadly. Our trial enrolled patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center. We have revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
