# Response to Reviewers

We thank both reviewers for their comments. Below we respond to each point; page and line numbers refer to the revised manuscript.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We have added group-specific loss to follow-up to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and to the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group. The original submission reported only the overall figure.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We have kept the 90-day endpoint and added the rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days [CITATION NEEDED: source for this proportion], and the intervention was designed to act during that transition period. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample size than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We agree and have narrowed the conclusions. The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center. The Discussion now limits the conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306), and we have added single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
