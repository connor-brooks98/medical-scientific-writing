Two findings deserve comment. First, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days across the 12 hospitals in this cluster-randomized trial — from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). Second, the effect was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points) [DATA NEEDED: 95% CI for this estimate, whether the subgroup was prespecified, and the test for interaction]. We cannot tell whether that difference reflects a greater response to reconciliation or the higher event rate in patients taking more medications [DATA NEEDED: event rates by arm within each subgroup].
