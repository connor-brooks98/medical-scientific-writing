## Discussion

In this retrospective cohort of 1,146 patients with dementia, mobilization within 24 hours of hip fracture surgery was associated with lower 30-day mortality (6.2% vs 9.8%; adjusted odds ratio, 0.61; 95% CI, 0.39–0.95) and shorter length of stay (median 8 vs 11 days). A prospective cohort recording prefracture mobility and dementia severity, or a trial randomizing the timing of first mobilization in patients with dementia, would separate the effect of mobilization from the baseline differences that make it possible.
