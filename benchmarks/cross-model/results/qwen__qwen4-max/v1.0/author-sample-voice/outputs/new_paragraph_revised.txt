Pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days across the 12 hospitals in this cluster-randomized trial — from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). That is about 36 fewer events per 1,000 patients discharged. In patients taking 10 or more medications, the risk difference was -6.1 percentage points [DATA NEEDED: 95% CI for this estimate, P for interaction, and whether the subgroup was prespecified]. We would not limit reconciliation to patients taking 10 or more medications on the strength of one subgroup estimate.
