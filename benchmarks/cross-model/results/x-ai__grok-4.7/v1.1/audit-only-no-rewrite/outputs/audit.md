# Audit: Introduction

Read as the introduction to a prospective study of early norepinephrine in septic shock, using one single-centre retrospective analysis as motivation. Journal not stated. No rewrite below; suggested fixes are directions only.

| Paragraph 1, sentence 1 | "Sepsis remains a leading cause of death worldwide" | D6 | Major | Cut this opening. It could start any sepsis paper and is uncited. Begin with the timing question this study asks. Keep a burden figure only if it is specific, cited, and tied to vasopressor timing. |
| Paragraph 1, sentence 1 | "representing a pivotal challenge in the evolving landscape of critical care" | S1, W1 | Minor | Delete the tail. It restates the opening clause and adds no fact. "Pivotal" and "evolving landscape" are empty. |

| Paragraph 1, sentence 3 | "Studies have shown that early norepinephrine improves survival in septic shock." | D2 | Critical | Name the studies (design, population, sample size, estimate) or delete the sentence. Do not insert a citation from memory. "Improves" is a causal verb; use it only for a randomized comparison of that endpoint. Otherwise the claim is unsupported. |
| Paragraph 1, sentence 4 | "Furthermore, a growing body of evidence suggests that delayed vasopressor therapy may potentially be associated with worse outcomes in some patients." | D2, D9, S6 | Critical | Delete. | Paragraph 2, sentence 1 | "norepinephrine started within 1 hour of shock onset was associated with lower 28-day mortality (adjusted odds ratio, 0.78; 95% CI, 0.64–0.94)" | S13 | Major | Name the reference group and the covariates. | Paragraph 2, sentence 2 | "Early norepinephrine therefore prevents death in septic shock." | S6, D8 | Critical | Remove this conclusion. An adjusted odds ratio from a retrospective cohort does not show that norepinephrine prevents death, and "therefore" makes the leap explicit. "Death" is also broader than 28-day mortality, and an odds ratio is not a risk ratio. Do not generalize one institution's association to septic shock at large. If a design limit belongs here, state it once (timing is entangled with severity and with other elements of care) and do not rebut it. |
| Paragraph 2, sentence 3 | "To address this critical gap, we designed a comprehensive prospective study that will not only fill an important gap in the literature but also pave the way for improved outcomes." | D4, D5, S3 | Major | The gap has no referent: the previous sentence already treats the question as settled. State the actual unknown, then the aim (population, timing contrast, primary outcome, hypothesis). Delete "not only...but also", "comprehensive", and "pave the way for improved outcomes". A study still being introduced cannot be said to improve outcomes. If the study is finished, put the aim in the past tense and do not preview a verdict. |

## Five revisions that would help most

1. Delete "Early norepinephrine therefore prevents death in septic shock." Keep the association language that already matches the retrospective design. This is the sentence that misleads.
2. Support or remove both external claims ("Studies have shown", "a growing body of evidence"). Match each verb to the cited design. Do not add references from memory; use a citation you have checked, or leave a placeholder.
3. Resolve the contradiction between a settled benefit and a "critical gap". One unanswered question, then what the prospective study compares. Cut the promise that the study will improve outcomes.
4. Replace the first two sentences. They never reach vasopressor timing, which is the only subject of the piece.
5. Make the odds ratio interpretable: reference group and adjustment set. Then delete the delayed-therapy sentence; it adds no second finding.

## Leave as is

- "Was associated with" in the retrospective sentence. That is the right verb for this design; do not weaken it into a hedge stack or strengthen it into a causal verb.
- The figures already in the text: 2,914; 1 hour; 28-day mortality; 0.78; 95% CI 0.64–0.94. The en dash in the interval is correct typography.
- No null result was available to restore. Only one result is in the draft.

## Author query

If the 2,914-patient analysis had other outcomes, they are not in this text, so this audit cannot judge selective reporting. The adjustment set and the comparison group are also unspecified.
