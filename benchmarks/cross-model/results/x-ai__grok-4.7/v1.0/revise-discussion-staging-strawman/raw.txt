## Discussion

In this retrospective cohort of 1,146 patients with dementia, mobilization within 24 hours of hip fracture surgery was associated with lower 30-day mortality (6.2% vs 9.8%; adjusted odds ratio, 0.61; 95% CI, 0.39–0.95). Length of stay was shorter in patients mobilized within 24 hours (median 8 vs 11 days). Delirium incidence, however, did not differ between groups (31% vs 29%).

Preoperative ambulatory status was not recorded for 18% of patients. If patients with missing status differed in walking ability from those with recorded status, the mortality, length-of-stay, and delirium comparisons could be biased in either direction.
