# Response to Reviewers

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We added group-specific counts to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 (5.0%) in the control group were lost to follow-up.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints during trial design. We chose 90 days because most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within that period and the intervention targets the transition after discharge. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample than our funding allowed. We retained the 90-day endpoint and added this rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125).

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306). We also added the trial’s single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
