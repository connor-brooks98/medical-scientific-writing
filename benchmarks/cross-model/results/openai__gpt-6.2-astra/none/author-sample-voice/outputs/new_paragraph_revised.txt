Two findings deserve comment. First, in this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced the risk of an adverse drug event within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, −3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, −5.9 to −1.3). Second, the benefit appeared larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, −6.1 percentage points). This pattern suggests that patients taking more medications may benefit more from reconciliation, but the subgroup estimate alone does not establish that the effect differed by medication count.
