## Discussion

On postoperative day (POD) 1, the surgical team attributed the patient's nausea, vomiting, and abdominal discomfort to ileus and opioid analgesia. Her capillary glucose was 182 mg/dL. When she developed tachypnea that evening, a venous blood gas showed acidemia (pH 7.12) with a bicarbonate of 9 mmol/L and an anion gap of 24 mmol/L. Lactate was 1.4 mmol/L. The serum beta-hydroxybutyrate concentration of 6.8 mmol/L supported the diagnosis of euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis (euDKA), which was made on the morning of POD 2, approximately 14 hours after the first abnormal blood gas.

Euglycemic DKA is an uncommon but potentially life-threatening complication of sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitor therapy. SGLT2 inhibitor use has been associated with up to a three-fold increase in DKA risk [CITATION NEEDED: risk estimate, study population, and comparator]. These drugs promote urinary glucose loss, so ketoacidosis can develop without marked hyperglycemia. Lower insulin requirements, reduced carbohydrate intake during fasting, and the stress of surgery can favor ketogenesis. In this patient, recent empagliflozin use and a 14-hour preoperative fast may both have contributed.

The FDA recommends withholding empagliflozin for at least 3 days before scheduled surgery [5]. The patient took her last dose on the morning before surgery. After diagnosis, she received intravenous insulin with 10% dextrose, fluids, and potassium replacement. Her anion gap closed after 38 hours. Empagliflozin was permanently discontinued, and she was discharged on POD 6 on metformin and basal insulin.

## Conclusion

In a postoperative patient with recent SGLT2 inhibitor use, nausea or vomiting accompanied by tachypnea or unexplained anion gap acidosis should prompt measurement of serum beta-hydroxybutyrate, even when blood glucose is not markedly elevated. For patients taking empagliflozin, preoperative medication review should establish when the last dose was taken, not just whether the drug will be held on the morning of surgery.
