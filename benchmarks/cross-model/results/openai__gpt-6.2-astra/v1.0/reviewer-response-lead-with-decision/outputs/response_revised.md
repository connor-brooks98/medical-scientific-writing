# Response to reviewers

Thank you for your comments.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** The original submission reported only the overall loss to follow-up. We added group-specific counts to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints during trial design. We chose 90 days because the intervention targeted the transition after discharge and most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample than our funding allowed. We retained the 90-day primary endpoint and added this rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125).

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306). We also added the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
