Two findings deserve comment. First, in this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). Second, the estimated reduction was larger among patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points) — though whether the effect differs by medication count is uncertain.
