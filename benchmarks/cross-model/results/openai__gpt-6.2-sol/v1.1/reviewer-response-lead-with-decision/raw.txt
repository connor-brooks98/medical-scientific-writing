# Response to reviewers

Thank you for your comments.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We added group-specific counts to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 (5.0%) in the control group. The original submission reported only the overall count.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We retained the 90-day primary endpoint and added the rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125). We considered both endpoints when designing the trial. Most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within the first 90 days, and the intervention was designed for the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** We revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306). We enrolled patients at a single academic center and now state the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
