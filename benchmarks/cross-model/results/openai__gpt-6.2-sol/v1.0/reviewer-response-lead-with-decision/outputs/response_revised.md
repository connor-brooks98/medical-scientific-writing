# Response to reviewers

Thank you for reviewing our manuscript.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** The original submission reported only overall loss to follow-up. We now report the counts by group in the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184): 14 patients (4.1%) in the intervention group and 17 patients (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints during trial design and retained the 90-day primary endpoint. In the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125), we explain that most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur in the first 90 days and that the intervention was designed for the transition period. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample than our funding allowed.

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at a single academic center. We revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and identified the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
