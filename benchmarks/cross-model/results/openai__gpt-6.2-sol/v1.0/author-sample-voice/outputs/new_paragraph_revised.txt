In this cluster-randomized trial across 12 hospitals, pharmacist-led medication reconciliation at discharge reduced adverse drug events within 30 days from 14.2% to 10.6% (risk difference, -3.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -5.9 to -1.3). The estimated reduction was larger in patients taking 10 or more medications (risk difference, -6.1 percentage points). That estimate alone does not tell us whether the intervention works better in these patients.
