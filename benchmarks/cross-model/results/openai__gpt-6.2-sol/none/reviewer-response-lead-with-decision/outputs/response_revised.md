# Response to Reviewers

**Reviewer 1, Comment 1:** The authors should report the number of patients lost to follow-up in each group.

**Response:** We added the group-specific numbers to the CONSORT diagram (Figure 1) and the Results (page 9, lines 182–184). We lost 14 patients (4.1%) to follow-up in the intervention group and 17 (5.0%) in the control group.

**Reviewer 1, Comment 2:** Please justify the choice of a 90-day primary endpoint rather than a 1-year endpoint.

**Response:** We considered both endpoints when designing the trial. We chose 90 days because most readmissions after heart failure hospitalization occur within that period and the intervention was designed for the transition after discharge. A 1-year endpoint would have required a larger sample than our funding allowed. We retained the 90-day endpoint and added this rationale to the Methods (page 6, lines 121–125).

**Reviewer 2, Comment 1:** The discussion overstates the generalizability of the findings to all patients with heart failure.

**Response:** The trial enrolled only patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction at one academic center. We revised the Discussion to limit our conclusions to patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction treated at academic centers (page 14, lines 301–306) and noted the single-center design as a limitation (page 15, lines 322–325).
